## Alpha Harness (SuperAlpha recipes)
`hypolab/superalpha.py` adapts the five selection recipes, four of the combo (weighting) recipes, the 8,000 / limit
operator cap, the default decay and truncation, and the shape of the SuperAlpha request from Alpha Harness,
`backend/src/alpha_harness/labs/super_alpha.py` and `backend/src/alpha_harness/brain/schemas.py`
(https://github.com/residual-lab/alpha-harness, commit of 2026-10-07, read 2026-10-20). The reasons given for each
recipe, the checks, the selection evaluator and everything else in the module are HYPOLAB's.
