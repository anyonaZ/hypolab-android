## House rules
- At most 4 alpha submissions a day; it is a ceiling, not a quota. Combined alpha performance is a Genius bar, so weak filler hurts.
- SuperAlphas unlock after 100 lifetime submitted alphas; then at most 1 a day on top of the alphas. HYPOLAB builds them in the cockpit (SuperAlpha tab) from recipes over pools of 10+ submitted alphas: the projects never write selection or combo code.
- Trial budget: 10 simulations per idea, then rethink. Every try is counted and the gate deflates the Sharpe by it.
- Submission bars (BRAIN's own checks are the authority): delay-1 Sharpe >= 1.58, fitness >= 1.0; self- and production correlation < 0.7.
- Expected turnover range for a healthy alpha: 5%-40%.
- AI writes hypotheses (story, mechanism, roles, ladder with placeholders); HYPOLAB chooses fields, lints, simulates and gates; Zacc approves and submits.
- An alpha counts toward at most 2 Pyramids: keep a card to 1-2 data categories (dividing by close/cap adds Price Volume).
- Power Pool shape (a route to the combined-performance bar): Sharpe >= 1.0, <= 8 operators (ts_backfill/group_backfill not counted), <= 3 fields, Power Pool correlation < 0.5.
- Region Agnostic Alphas (USA/EUR/ASI/GLB in one simulation, D1) use one submission but count every passing region as a signal and in its Pyramid: prefer data that exists in several regions.
- Neutralization to try first (BRAIN): fundamental/analyst/earnings/short interest INDUSTRY, news SUBINDUSTRY, option/price-volume MARKET or SECTOR, sentiment/insiders INDUSTRY or SUBINDUSTRY.
