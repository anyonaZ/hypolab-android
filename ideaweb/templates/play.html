Why the dice still count

Random search finds luck as easily as signal: try 40 noise fields and one will look good. So every roll is journaled as a try for its idea (play:<card> or play:wild:<dataset>). If a roll goes to the Lab and the gate, the deflated Sharpe counts all the dice you threw on that idea. A good roll is a lead: before you submit, ask what the economic story is - then it can become a proper card.
