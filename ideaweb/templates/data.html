Learn a dataset: explore, then scan

  1. Explore (free): each usable field gets only the lenses that make sense for its nature - a cash-flow amount becomes a yield, an estimate becomes a revision, a count a relative change.
  2. Scan (one simulation per field, all journaled): every field probed once. The noise bar is the |Sharpe| the luckiest pure-noise field would reach; only fields above it are candidates.

A scan finds where to look, not alphas. If you later build an alpha from a scanned field, the gate counts every field you scanned as a try: choosing the best of 40 is a 40-way selection.
