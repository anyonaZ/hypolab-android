A sensible daily routine (about 2 hours)
  1. 20 min - read. The daily alpha brief (17:55 Nairobi), one paper abstract from a card's references, or one BRAIN Learn page.
  2. 10 min - plan. Week (today's submissions, the plan) and Pyramids (which cells this week).
  3. 15 min - ideas. Leads: act on one lead or one outside source; import the Writer's card on Drafts, check it, copy it for the Critic, approve it.
  4. 50 min - research. Cards › Send to the Lab 2-3 cards for this week's cells › Lab: rung 1, read the diagnosis, climb at most a few rungs. Respect the stop signs.
  5. 20 min - validate. Lab: production correlation (free), robustness and the gate on anything that passed. Submit only what clears the gate (at most 4 a day). After 100 lifetime alphas: one SuperAlpha a day at most, from a pool of 10+.
  6. 5 min - write. One lesson per idea in the Journal. Fridays: the DIGEST / PLAN review on the Week page.
